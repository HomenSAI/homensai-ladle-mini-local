# Lädle Mini Local — Quick guide

An offline order book for one Android phone or tablet. No server, customer accounts, or cloud. The app supports English, German, and Russian, plus four visual themes using the same data.

**Delivery status:** Source project and browser preview; no installable APK yet. Android build and device acceptance have not been run. Use fictional data until Android acceptance is complete.

Set up the shop → create a pickup date → confirm products, units, and prices → record customer preorders → review item totals under **Prepare** → record actual collection → record payment separately → close the date.

The catalog contains 91 editable templates. 51 are active by default across young beef, beef, lamb, chicken, wild boar, and deer. Rabbit and other groups are initially disabled. No prices are preset. Edit or rename templates, change their availability, and add your own photos. Used templates are archived when removed; historical orders remain intact.

Units are kg, pieces, packs, and litres. Pieces and packs must be whole numbers. Different units are never added together as kilograms. For a partial collection, leave **Collection complete** unchecked and enter the cumulative amount already handed over next time. Additional items sold at pickup can be recorded without changing the preorder.

Enter the **total amount received so far** for payment. For example, if €20 was received and then another €10, enter €30. The app does not process card payments and is not a cash register. Unsaved forms may be recovered as drafts; drafts are separate from confirmed data. Keep encrypted backups somewhere outside the phone and test restoring one. Do not uninstall the app or clear its data until a backup has been verified.
